How to get started (free)
  1. Get a key: open aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with Google and click Create API key.
  2. Paste it above and press Save. We check it with Google straight away.
  3. Translate: open your English video in another tab, come back here and press the round button.

Your key is only sent straight from your browser to Google. This site has no server and never sees it.
